Every morning

GO, MODIFY or REST, in ten seconds

Generic readiness scores compare you to everyone. Coach Hero compares last night to your last 60 days, factor by factor, so you see what is off and why.

  • One bad night isn't a verdict. Three short nights in a week is, even when each looks normal.
  • A specific change, not a vague warning. Drop the top set, make the intervals easy, or move the test to Friday.
  • Your watch updates itself. When today's plan changes, so does the workout on your wrist.

What makes it a coach, not a workout generator

Your baseline, not averages

Every flag is relative to your own history. A resting HR of 60 means something different for you than for anyone else.

Trends over snapshots

It acts on patterns that repeat, not on one strange night, and says how sure it is.

Plans that change with you

Missed sessions, a busy week, a new goal: next week is rebuilt from the evidence, and the change is explained.

Works around injuries

Tell it what hurts. It swaps movements, caps loads, and checks in until it's resolved.

Any gym, any kit

Commercial gym, home rack, a pair of dumbbells or just a pull-up bar. Weights are rounded to what you can actually load.

Reviewed before it starts

Each new block is checked for programming, recovery, injury risk and nutrition before it reaches your watch.

Questions

When can I use it?

We're building the app now and plan to open a beta first. Ask for early access and you'll hear from us as soon as it's ready. The open-source version works today on your computer.

Which watches does it work with?

Any Garmin watch that syncs with Garmin Connect. What it can read depends on your watch: HRV, for example, needs a model that measures it overnight. Coach Hero only uses what your watch records and never makes up a missing number.

Do I need to be an athlete?

No. It plans for where you are now, from a first 5k or a first pull-up to a competitive lift. Easier at the start of a block, harder toward the end, with rest built in.

What happens to my data?

Your training and health data is used to coach you, and for nothing else. We never sell it, and you can delete it at any time.

Is it medical advice?

No. Coach Hero is a training coach. If something hurts or feels wrong, it tells you to get it checked, and it plans around injuries rather than through them.
