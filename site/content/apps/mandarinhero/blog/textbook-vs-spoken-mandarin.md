Textbooks teach correct Chinese. They don't always teach what people actually *say*. Here are ten swaps that make you sound less like a textbook and more like someone who lives there. Most of the "real" examples come straight from Mandarin Hero's lessons.

## 1. Greeting: skip 你好吗

**Textbook:** 你好吗？ *nǐ hǎo ma?* — How are you?

**Real:** a simple **嗨** (*hāi*) or **你好**, the person's name, or **最近怎么样？** (*zuìjìn zěnmeyàng?*, "how have things been?"). Older people may ask **吃了吗？** (*chī le ma?*, "have you eaten?"). It's a greeting, not a dinner invitation.

## 2. Saying yes: 对, not 是

**Textbook:** 是 (*shì*) for "yes".

**Real:** **对** (*duì*, "right") when confirming something: 你是小王的朋友吧？ — **对**，你好！ Even **对对对** when you're agreeing enthusiastically.

## 3. Goodbye: 拜拜

**Textbook:** 再见 *zàijiàn*

**Real:** **拜拜** (*bàibài*) with friends, and **先走了** (*xiān zǒu le*, "I'm off") when leaving. 再见 is fine, just a bit more formal.

## 4. You're welcome: 没事儿

**Textbook:** 不客气 *bú kèqi*

**Real:** **没事** or **没事儿** (*méishì(r)*, "no problem"), often together: **没事儿，不客气。**

## 5. I don't understand: 听不懂

**Textbook:** 我不明白 *wǒ bù míngbai*

**Real:** when you can't follow what someone *said*: **我听不懂** (*wǒ tīng bu dǒng*, "I can't understand what I'm hearing"). Add **不好意思** first and it's polite too. 明白 is more for understanding an idea: **明白了！** ("Got it!").

## 6. Very good: 挺好的

**Textbook:** 很好 *hěn hǎo*

**Real:** **挺好的** (*tǐng hǎo de*, "pretty good, really nice") or **不错** (*búcuò*, "not bad", which means good). At the barber: **挺好的，谢谢！**

## 7. Ordering: 来, not 我想要

**Textbook:** 我想要… *wǒ xiǎng yào…*

**Real:** **我要…** or just **来…** (*lái*, "bring"): **来两瓶啤酒**, "two beers, please." Shorter and perfectly polite.

## 8. OK: 行 and 好嘞

**Textbook:** 好的 *hǎo de*

**Real:** 好的 is common, and so are **行** (*xíng*, "fine, works for me") and the cheerful **好嘞** (*hǎo lei*, "you got it!") from waiters and drivers.

## 9. Taking a compliment: 没有没有

**Textbook:** 谢谢 (thanks), or the old-fashioned 哪里哪里 (*nǎlǐ nǎlǐ*)

**Real:** compliments get politely batted away. **没有没有，还差得远呢** means "Nah, I've still got a long way to go." Expect your Chinese to be complimented. Now you know the answer.

## 10. Questions: add a particle

**Textbook:** 你是哪国人？ *nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?*

**Real:** **你是哪国人啊？** That little **啊** makes it sound curious and friendly instead of like a form. More in [啊, 呢, 吧 and 嘛 explained](/apps/mandarinhero/blog/chinese-particles-a-ne-ba-ma/).

## Why textbooks sound like textbooks

Textbooks aim for language that's correct everywhere, in writing too. Speech is shorter, warmer and full of particles. You don't need to throw away what you learned. Just add the spoken versions, and hear them often enough that they come out naturally.
