HSK (汉语水平考试, *Hànyǔ Shuǐpíng Kǎoshì*) is the official Chinese proficiency test, and its word lists are the backbone of most courses. But what does "HSK 3" actually mean when you're standing in a shop? Here's a practical breakdown.

## The six levels at a glance

| Level | Words | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|
| HSK 1 | 150 | Greetings, numbers, "I want this one", "how much?" |
| HSK 2 | 300 | Simple everyday exchanges: shopping, time, directions |
| HSK 3 | 600 | Basic conversations about daily life, work and travel |
| HSK 4 | 1,200 | Talk about a fairly wide range of topics with some fluency |
| HSK 5 | 2,500 | Follow TV and films, give a short talk, read newspapers with effort |
| HSK 6 | 5,000 | Understand most of what you hear and read, and express yourself fluently |

Each level includes all the words of the levels below. HSK 6 is the full 5,000.

## What the levels don't tell you

**The HSK exam mostly tests listening and reading.** Writing comes in from HSK 3. Speaking has a separate exam, the **HSKK**. So it's quite possible to pass HSK 4 and still freeze in a real conversation, or to chat happily at a market without ever having taken a test.

**The word lists are written-register heavy.** They include many words you'll mostly read, not say. Meanwhile, everyday spoken words and particles, like 吧, 嘛, 好嘞 and 没事儿, barely feature. (See [what natives actually say](/apps/mandarinhero/blog/textbook-vs-spoken-mandarin/).)

**Knowing a word isn't the same as using it.** Recognising 打包 on a card is one thing. Saying 剩下的帮我们打包一下 at the end of a meal is another.

## How HSK maps to speaking

As a rough guide, if you've *practised speaking* alongside the words:

- **HSK 1–2:** you can survive. Order food, take a taxi, buy things, introduce yourself.
- **HSK 3:** you can chat. Simple conversations about your day, your work, your plans.
- **HSK 4:** you can hold a conversation with friends on most everyday topics.
- **HSK 5–6:** you can follow natives talking among themselves, and join in.

## The new HSK (3.0)

In 2021 a new standard was published with **nine levels in three bands** and a considerably bigger word list. It's being phased in alongside the six-level exam. If you're planning to take an exam, check the official HSK website for which version applies.

## Should you study for the HSK?

- **If you need the certificate** for study, a scholarship or a job: yes, and practise in the exam format.
- **If you want to speak:** use the HSK lists as a map of useful words, but learn them in phrases and say them out loud. The test can come later, or never.
