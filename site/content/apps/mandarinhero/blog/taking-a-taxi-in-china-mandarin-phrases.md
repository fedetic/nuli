Taxis and ride-hailing (**滴滴**, *Dīdī*, DiDi) are the easiest way to get around a Chinese city, and the conversation is short and predictable. Here's everything you need, from [Mandarin Hero](/apps/mandarinhero/)'s taxi lesson.

## Call the driver 师傅

**师傅** (*shīfu*) literally means "master". It's how everyone addresses drivers, mechanics and other skilled workers. It's polite, friendly and never wrong.

## Getting in: confirm it's your ride

With DiDi, the driver checks you're the right passenger with the **last four digits of your phone number**, **手机尾号** (*shǒujī wěihào*):

> 去国贸的是吧？手机尾号多少？ — Going to Guomao, right? What are the last digits of your phone?
>
> 三八五六。 — 3856.
>
> 对，上车吧。 — That's it, hop in.

Read digits one at a time, and remember that 1 is often said **幺** (*yāo*) in phone numbers.

In a street taxi, show the address and say:

- **师傅，去这个地址。** *Shīfu, qù zhège dìzhǐ.* — To this address, please.
- **打表吗？** *Dǎbiǎo ma?* — Do you use the meter?

## Phrases for the ride

| Phrase | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 能开一下后备箱吗？ | Néng kāi yíxià hòubèixiāng ma? | Could you open the boot/trunk? |
| 大概要多久？ | Dàgài yào duōjiǔ? | About how long will it take? |
| 能开一下空调吗？ | Néng kāi yíxià kōngtiáo ma? | Could you turn on the air con? |
| 我赶时间，麻烦快一点。 | Wǒ gǎn shíjiān, máfan kuài yìdiǎn. | I'm in a hurry, a bit faster please. |
| 前面路口停就行。 | Qiánmiàn lùkǒu tíng jiù xíng. | Just stop at the next intersection. |
| 就在这儿停吧。 | Jiù zài zhèr tíng ba. | Stop right here, please. |

Notice **就行** (*jiù xíng*, "is fine") and **一下** (*yíxià*, "a bit, briefly"). They soften requests and make you sound natural instead of bossy.

## Small talk

Drivers love to chat. Expect this one:

> 你是哪国人啊？中文说得不错嘛。 — Where are you from? Your Chinese isn't bad.
>
> 荷兰人。还在学呢。 — Dutch. Still learning.

That **嘛** and **呢** are particles doing real work. See [啊, 呢, 吧 and 嘛 explained](/apps/mandarinhero/blog/chinese-particles-a-ne-ba-ma/).

## When the driver can't find you

> 喂，你在哪儿呢？我在门口没看到你。 — Hello? Where are you? I'm at the entrance and can't see you.
>
> 我在麦当劳门口，穿蓝色衣服。 — I'm outside McDonald's, wearing blue.

Describing a landmark and what you're wearing solves this every time.

## Getting out and paying

> 快到了，停在哪个门？ — Nearly there. Which entrance should I stop at?
>
> 前面路口停就行，我走过去。 — Just stop at the intersection ahead. I'll walk.
>
> 好嘞。钱在手机上付就行，东西别落下。 — Sure thing. You can pay on your phone. Don't leave anything behind.
>
> 好，谢谢师傅！ — OK, thanks!

With DiDi you pay in the app, and you get the receipt there too: **滴滴的发票在App里开就行**.

## Before your first ride

- Have your destination **written in Chinese** to show the driver. Hotel names in English often mean nothing to them.
- Practise saying your **phone digits** out loud until they come without thinking.
- Learn **师傅**, **就行** and **谢谢师傅**. That covers most of the ride.
