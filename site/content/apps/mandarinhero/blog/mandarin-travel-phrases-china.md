You don't need a thousand words for a trip to China. You need the right phrases at the right moments. Here they are, in the order you'll meet them, from Mandarin Hero's travel lessons.

## Immigration

> 来中国做什么？ — What brings you to China?
>
> 来旅游的。 — I'm here on holiday.
>
> 待多久？ — How long are you staying?
>
> 两个星期左右。 — About two weeks.

Also useful:

- **这是我的护照。** zhè shì wǒ de hùzhào. — Here's my passport.
- **外国人走哪边？** wàiguó rén zǒu nǎbiān? — Which way for foreigners?
- **没有要申报的。** méiyǒu yào shēnbào de. — Nothing to declare.

You'll also hear **按指纹** (*àn zhǐwén*, "fingerprints") and **看一下镜头** (*kàn yíxià jìngtóu*, "look at the camera").

## Hotel

- **你好，我要办入住。** nǐ hǎo, wǒ yào bàn rùzhù. — Hi, I'd like to check in.
- **押金多少？** yājīn duōshǎo? — How much is the deposit?
- **早餐几点到几点？** zǎocān jǐ diǎn dào jǐ diǎn? — What time is breakfast?
- **WiFi密码是多少？** WiFi mìmǎ shì duōshǎo? — What's the WiFi password?
- **行李能先寄存一下吗？** xíngli néng xiān jìcún yíxià ma? — Can I leave my bags here for now?

Many hotels take a **押金** (*yājīn*), a deposit you get back at checkout.

## Taxis and the metro

- **师傅，去这个地址。** shīfu, qù zhège dìzhǐ. — To this address, please. (Show it in Chinese.)
- **前面路口停就行。** qiánmiàn lùkǒu tíng jiù xíng. — Just stop at the next intersection.
- **去火车站坐几号线？** qù huǒchēzhàn zuò jǐ hào xiàn? — Which line for the train station?
- **在哪儿换乘？** zài nǎr huànchéng? — Where do I change?

## Paying

Almost everything is **扫码** (*sǎo mǎ*, "scan the code"):

- **可以用支付宝吗？** kěyǐ yòng Zhīfùbǎo ma? — Can I pay with Alipay?
- **我扫你还是你扫我？** wǒ sǎo nǐ háishi nǐ sǎo wǒ? — Do I scan you, or you scan me?
- **可以用现金吗？** kěyǐ yòng xiànjīn ma? — Can I pay cash?
- **一共多少钱？** yígòng duōshǎo qián? — How much altogether?

## When you don't understand

These may be the most important of all:

- **不好意思，我听不懂。** bù hǎoyìsi, wǒ tīng bu dǒng. — Sorry, I don't understand.
- **你能说慢一点吗？** nǐ néng shuō màn yìdiǎn ma? — Could you speak a bit slower?
- **能再说一遍吗？** néng zài shuō yí biàn ma? — Could you say that again?
- **我迷路了。** wǒ mílù le. — I'm lost.

## At the pharmacy

- **我拉肚子了，有止泻药吗？** wǒ lā dùzi le, yǒu zhǐxiè yào ma? — I've got diarrhoea. Do you have anything for it?
- **有创可贴吗？** yǒu chuāngkětiē ma? — Do you have plasters?
- **这个一天吃几次？** zhège yì tiān chī jǐ cì? — How many times a day do I take this?

## Before you go

- Save your **hotel address in Chinese** on your phone.
- Install **Alipay or WeChat Pay** and link your card before you leave.
- Practise the phrases above **out loud**. In the moment, they need to come out without thinking.
