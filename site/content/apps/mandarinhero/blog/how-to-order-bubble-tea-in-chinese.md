Bubble tea is one of the most satisfying first conversations to have in Chinese. It's short and predictable, and you get a drink at the end. The questions always come in the same order, so once you know them you'll understand every step.

## The questions you'll be asked

| They ask | Pinyin | Meaning | You answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 喝点什么？ | hē diǎn shénme? | What can I get you? | 我要一杯珍珠奶茶。 |
| 大杯还是中杯？ | dà bēi háishi zhōng bēi? | Large or medium? | 大杯吧。 |
| 甜度呢？ | tiándù ne? | How sweet? | 三分糖吧。 |
| 冰的还是热的？ | bīng de háishi rè de? | Iced or hot? | 冰的，少冰。 |
| 要加料吗？ | yào jiā liào ma? | Any toppings? | 不用了，谢谢。 |
| 在这儿喝还是带走？ | zài zhèr hē háishi dàizǒu? | For here or to go? | 带走，谢谢。 |

Notice the pattern: **A 还是 B?** (*háishi*) means "A or B?". Just repeat the one you want, and add 吧 (*ba*) to make it sound relaxed.

## Sugar: count in tenths

Shops measure sweetness in tenths (分, *fēn*):

- **全糖** quán táng — full sugar
- **七分糖** qī fēn táng — 70%
- **五分糖** wǔ fēn táng, or **半糖** bàn táng — 50%
- **三分糖** sān fēn táng — 30%
- **不加糖** bù jiā táng — no added sugar

Full sugar is *very* sweet. Many locals go for 三分糖, and **别太甜** (*bié tài tián*, "not too sweet") is a handy add-on.

## Ice

- **正常冰** zhèngcháng bīng — normal ice
- **少冰** shǎo bīng — less ice
- **去冰** qù bīng — no ice (still cold)
- **常温** chángwēn — room temperature
- **热的** rè de — hot

Not every drink can be made hot. If it can't, you'll hear **这款不能做热的** (*zhè kuǎn bù néng zuò rè de*, "this one can't be made hot").

## Toppings

**加料** (*jiā liào*) means adding toppings. The classics are **珍珠** (*zhēnzhū*, tapioca pearls), **椰果** (*yēguǒ*, coconut jelly) and **布丁** (*bùdīng*, pudding). To ask for one: **可以加椰果吗？** (*kěyǐ jiā yēguǒ ma?*, "Can I add coconut jelly?").

## The whole order, the way it really goes

This is the conversation from Mandarin Hero's bubble tea lesson:

> 你好，喝点什么？也可以扫码点单。 — Hi, what can I get you? You can also scan to order.
>
> 我就在这儿点吧。你们家招牌是什么？ — I'll just order here. What's your signature drink?
>
> 黑糖珍珠鲜奶，芝士葡萄也卖得很好。 — Brown sugar pearls with fresh milk. The cheese-foam grape tea sells well too.
>
> 那要一杯黑糖珍珠鲜奶。 — Then one brown sugar pearl milk, please.
>
> 大杯还是中杯？ — Large or medium?
>
> 大杯吧。 — Large, please.
>
> 甜度呢？全糖、七分、五分、三分都有。 — How sweet? Full, 70%, 50% or 30%.
>
> 三分糖吧，别太甜。 — 30%, please. Not too sweet.
>
> 冰的还是热的？ — Iced or hot?
>
> 冰的，少冰。 — Iced, but less ice.
>
> 在这儿喝还是带走？ — For here or to go?
>
> 带走，谢谢。 — To go, thanks.
>
> 一共十八。这是你的取餐号，三十二号，好了会叫你。 — That's 18. Here's your order number, 32. We'll call you when it's ready.

## The short version

If you only remember one line, make it this:

**大杯，少冰，半糖。** *Dà bēi, shǎo bīng, bàn táng.* — Large, less ice, half sugar.

It answers three questions at once, and staff hear it all day.

Then listen for your number: **三十二号！** (*sānshí'èr hào*). If it's taking a while, you can ask **三十二号好了吗？** ("Is number 32 ready?").
