Eating out is half the fun of being in China, and the conversation follows the same steps every time: a table, the menu, a few questions, the bill. Here's each step, from Mandarin Hero's restaurant lesson.

## 1. Getting a table

> 欢迎光临！几位？ — Welcome! How many?
>
> 两位。 — Two.

**几位？** (*jǐ wèi?*) is the polite "how many people?". Answer with the number and **位** (*wèi*): 两位, 三位, 四位.

If it's busy, you'll hear 现在没位子，要等二十分钟左右 ("No tables right now, about a twenty-minute wait"). Answer **没事，我们等一下** ("No problem, we'll wait").

## 2. The menu: scan or paper

Most restaurants now have you **扫码点餐** (*sǎo mǎ diǎn cān*): scan the QR code on the table and order on your phone. If you'd rather not:

- **有菜单吗？** *Yǒu càidān ma?* — Do you have a menu?
- **有纸的菜单吗？** *Yǒu zhǐ de càidān ma?* — Do you have a paper menu?

## 3. Ordering

- **你们这儿什么最好吃？** *Nǐmen zhèr shénme zuì hǎochī?* — What's best here?
- **招牌菜** *zhāopáicài* — the signature dish. Usually a safe bet.
- **辣不辣？** *Là bu là?* — Is it spicy?
- **微辣就行。** *Wēi là jiù xíng.* — Just a little spicy is fine.
- **不要放香菜。** *Bú yào fàng xiāngcài.* — No coriander, please.
- **再来一碗米饭。** *Zài lái yì wǎn mǐfàn.* — Another bowl of rice, please.

**来** (*lái*, literally "come") is the natural way to order: **来两瓶啤酒** means "two beers, please".

## 4. Allergies

Say it clearly with **我对 … 过敏** (*wǒ duì … guòmǐn*):

> 对了，我对花生过敏，这个菜里有花生吗？ — Oh, and I'm allergic to peanuts. Are there any in this?
>
> 上面一般会放一点，我跟厨房说一声，不放花生。 — There are usually a few on top. I'll tell the kitchen to leave them out.

## 5. During the meal

- **服务员！** *Fúwùyuán!* — Excuse me! (to call the waiter; normal and not rude)
- **我们的菜还没上。** *Wǒmen de cài hái méi shàng.* — Our food hasn't come yet.
- **慢用！** *Màn yòng!* — Enjoy! (what staff say when the food arrives)

## 6. Paying and taking leftovers

- **服务员，买单！** *Fúwùyuán, mǎidān!* — The bill, please!
- **扫这个码就行。** *Sǎo zhège mǎ jiù xíng.* — Just scan this code. (what you'll hear)
- **剩下的帮我们打包一下。** *Shèngxià de bāng wǒmen dǎbāo yíxià.* — Could you pack up the leftovers for us?

Taking leftovers home (**打包**, *dǎbāo*) is completely normal, so don't be shy.

## The whole meal in one conversation

> 欢迎光临！几位？ — Welcome! How many?
>
> 两位。 — Two.
>
> 这边请。桌上扫码点餐就行。 — This way. Just scan the code on the table to order.
>
> 有纸的菜单吗？我手机没电了。 — Do you have a paper menu? My phone's dead.
>
> 有的，给您。 — Sure, here you go.
>
> 你们这儿什么最好吃？ — What's best here?
>
> 水煮鱼是我们的招牌菜，很多人点。 — The fish in chilli oil is our signature dish. Lots of people order it.
>
> 辣不辣？ — Is it spicy?
>
> 有点辣，可以做微辣。 — A bit. We can make it mild.
>
> 那就微辣吧，不要香菜。再来两碗米饭。 — Mild then, no coriander. And two bowls of rice.
>
> 好的。喝点什么？ — Sure. Anything to drink?
>
> 两瓶冰啤酒。 — Two cold beers.
>
> 好嘞，马上来！ — You got it, coming right up!
