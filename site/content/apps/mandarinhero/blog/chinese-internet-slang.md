Textbook Chinese won't prepare you for your first WeChat group. Your friends won't write 这很有趣 ("this is very interesting"). They'll write **笑死** and send a sticker. Here's the slang you'll meet most often, taken from Mandarin Hero's "Chatting with a friend" lesson.

## Reactions

| Slang | Pinyin | Meaning | Like saying |
|---|---|---|---|
| 笑死 | xiàosǐ | laughing to death | LOL, I'm dying |
| 无语 | wúyǔ | speechless (annoyed) | I can't even, ugh |
| 离谱 | lípǔ | off the charts, absurd | that's ridiculous |
| 绝了 | jué le | the ultimate | amazing, the best |
| 好家伙 | hǎo jiāhuo | good fellow | whoa, my goodness |
| 上头 | shàngtóu | goes to your head | addictive |

**笑死** is everywhere: on its own, as 哈哈哈，笑死, or as **笑死我了！** ("I'm dying!").

**无语** literally means "without words", but it's always exasperated: 我今天又要加班，无语 ("Overtime again today. Ugh.").

**离谱** is for anything absurd: **太离谱了吧！** ("That's ridiculous!").

**绝了** is the opposite, for something so good there's nothing above it: 我发现一家火锅，绝了 ("I found a hotpot place. It's amazing.").

## Work and life

- **摸鱼** mōyú — "touching fish": slacking off at work. **上班摸鱼中** ("Slacking off at work right now") is a classic status.
- **加班** jiābān — working overtime. Not slang, but you'll need it to complain.
- **卷** juǎn — "rolled up", short for 内卷 *nèijuǎn*: the rat race, everyone overworking to keep up. **太卷了** means "it's so competitive".
- **躺平** tǎngpíng — "lying flat": opting out of that rat race.

## Social life

- **吃瓜** chīguā — "eating melon": watching drama unfold as a spectator. 你就是来吃瓜的 means "you're just here for the gossip".
- **搭子** dāzi — a buddy for one specific activity: a 饭搭子 (meal buddy), 健身搭子 (gym buddy) or 游泳搭子 (swimming buddy).
- **脱单** tuōdān — "escaping single": getting into a relationship.
- **走起！** zǒuqǐ! — "Let's go!", "Let's do it!"

## How it sounds in a real chat

> 在干嘛？ — What are you up to?
>
> 上班摸鱼中，哈哈。你呢？ — Slacking off at work, haha. You?
>
> 我今天又要加班，无语。 — Overtime again today. Ugh.
>
> 好家伙，这周第三次了吧？ — Whoa, that's the third time this week, isn't it?
>
> 对啊，太卷了。我想躺平。 — Yep, it's such a rat race. I want to lie flat.
>
> 哈哈，周末出来放松一下？ — Haha, come out and relax this weekend?
>
> 好啊！我发现一家火锅，绝了。 — Yes! I've found a hotpot place. It's amazing.
>
> 走起！周六晚上？ — Let's do it! Saturday evening?

Notice the particles in there too, like 对啊 and 这周第三次了吧. If they're new to you, read [our guide to 啊, 呢, 吧 and 嘛](/apps/mandarinhero/blog/chinese-particles-a-ne-ba-ma/).

## Use it, but read the room

Slang lands well with friends and in group chats. Natives love it when a learner gets it right. With your boss, your landlord or anyone you've just met, stick to plain, polite Chinese.
