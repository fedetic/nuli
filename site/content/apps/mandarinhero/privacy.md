*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

Mandarin Hero is made by Nuli (nuli.nl). This policy says what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: info@nuli.nl.

## What we collect

**Your account.** On first launch the app makes an anonymous account for your phone, with a random id and no personal details. If you sign up, we store your email address and a hashed password (never the password itself).

**Your study progress.** Your cards' schedule, review history and settings are synced to our server, so they're backed up and follow your account to another phone.

**Purchases.** Hero Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat checks your purchases for us, under your account's random id, and receives the store's purchase record (product, price, dates).

**Blog emails.** If you sign up for new blog posts on nuli.nl, we store your email address and the language you chose, and email you each new post. Every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops them straight away.

**Usage statistics.** To see how the app is used and what to improve, Mandarin Hero sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog (EU servers): which screens and features you use, study sessions, and your device type, OS, app version, language and approximate location (country and city, derived from your IP address, which is then discarded). For new users, the first session is recorded as a screen recording with all text and images masked. None of this is linked to your account or email. You can switch it off any time in Profile → Settings → Account → "Share usage stats".

**Crash reports.** When the app crashes or freezes, it sends a crash report to Sentry (EU servers): where in the code it went wrong, and your device type, OS and app version. It contains no account, email or study content.

**Ad measurement, only if you allow it.** We advertise Mandarin Hero on Instagram and Facebook. When you finish setting up the app, it asks once whether it may tell Meta that you installed it (on iPhone, Apple's "Allow tracking" question). Only if you allow it: Meta's software in the app tells Meta that the app was installed and opened, with your device's advertising identifier, and RevenueCat tells Meta when you start a free trial or buy Hero Pro (product, price and date). Meta uses this to measure which of our ads work and to show them to people like you. Nothing about what you study is shared. If you don't allow it, nothing is sent to Meta. You can change your answer any time: on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, on Android in Profile → Settings → Preferences → "Ad measurement".

The app doesn't show ads, and we never sell your data or share it with data brokers. The app doesn't use your microphone, contacts or precise location. Study reminders are scheduled on your phone itself.

## Who processes it

- **Railway** hosts our server and database.
- **RevenueCat** handles purchase validation.
- **PostHog** (EU) handles usage statistics.
- **Sentry** (EU) handles crash reports.
- **Meta** receives ad measurement, only if you allow it, under [Meta's privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).
- **Resend** sends our emails: account codes and blog posts.
- **Apple** and **Google** handle payments under their own privacy policies.

Each processes data only on our behalf, except Meta, which uses it under its own policy as well.

## How long we keep it

Your account and synced progress are kept until you delete your account. Usage statistics are kept for up to one year, screen recordings for 30 days and crash reports for up to 90 days; they aren't tied to your account, so deleting your account doesn't remove them. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for the stores' and tax rules. What Meta receives is kept by Meta under its own policy. Blog sign-ups are kept until you ask us to remove them; after you unsubscribe, we keep only your address marked as unsubscribed, so it's never emailed again.

## Your rights

You can delete your account and its synced progress at any time in the app (Profile → Settings → Account → "Delete account") or by email, see [Support](/apps/mandarinhero/support/). Under the GDPR you can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to stop processing it, by emailing info@nuli.nl, and you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Mandarin Hero isn't directed at children under 13.

We'll post changes to this policy on this page.
