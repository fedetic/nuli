*Last updated: 29 October 2026 (draft)*

Coach Hero is made by Nuli (nuli.nl). This policy says what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: info@nuli.nl.

## What we collect

**Your account.** On first launch the app makes an anonymous account for your phone, with a random id and no personal details. If you sign up, we store your email address and a hashed password (never the password itself).

**Your Garmin data.** When you connect Garmin, you sign in on Garmin's own page; your Garmin password never reaches us. We receive and store, encrypted, Garmin's login key for your account, and read from Garmin Connect what your coach needs: your profile (age, sex, height, weight, units, time zone), your watch, your activities and their sets, sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, stress, Body Battery, training readiness, VO2max and body weight. The coach also puts workouts on your Garmin calendar and watch. This is health data: connecting Garmin is your consent to us using it to coach you, and disconnecting (Settings → Garmin) withdraws it.

**What you tell your coach.** Your answers in onboarding, your goals, injuries, schedule, equipment and food habits, your messages to the coach, your morning-check answers, and the plan, check-ins and notes the coach keeps.

**The coach.** Your coach is an AI. To answer you, the relevant parts of the above are sent to Anthropic's Claude models, which process them for us and don't use them to train their models. Its advice is about training, not medical advice.

**Purchases.** The subscription is bought through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat checks your purchases for us, under your account's random id, and receives the store's purchase record (product, price, dates).

**Usage statistics.** To see how the app is used and what to improve, Coach Hero sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog (EU servers): which screens and features you use, the steps of onboarding, and your device type, OS, app version, language and approximate location (country and city, derived from your IP address, which is then discarded). None of this is linked to your account or email.

We don't use advertising identifiers, show ads, track you across other apps, or sell or share your data with data brokers. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself.

## Who processes it

- **Railway** hosts our server and database.
- **Anthropic** runs the AI models your coach uses.
- **Garmin** provides your data from Garmin Connect, under its own privacy policy.
- **RevenueCat** handles purchase validation.
- **PostHog** (EU) handles usage statistics.
- **Apple** and **Google** handle payments under their own privacy policies.

Each processes data only on our behalf, except Garmin, Apple and Google for their own services.

## How long we keep it

Your account, Garmin data and coaching history are kept until you delete your account. Disconnecting Garmin deletes its login key at once. Usage statistics are kept for up to one year; they aren't tied to your account, so deleting your account doesn't remove them. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for the stores' and tax rules.

## Your rights

You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time in the app (Settings → "Delete account"). Under the GDPR you can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to stop processing it, by emailing info@nuli.nl, and you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Coach Hero isn't directed at anyone under 16.

We'll post changes to this policy on this page.
