title: KnowYourPlate - Account & Data Deletion
---

## Delete your account in-app (instant)

1. Open KnowYourPlate
2. Go to the Profile tab
3. Scroll to the bottom > tap "Delete account"

This permanently deletes your account and all associated data immediately.

## Request deletion by email

Email nulilifestyle@gmail.com with subject "Account Deletion Request"
and include the email address linked to your account.
Requests are processed within 30 days.
